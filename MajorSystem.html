The Major System in 60 seconds

Each digit maps to a consonant sound (not a letter). Vowels and the consonants w, h, y are free filler, so any number can be spoken as a word: 32 → M‑N → “moon”. Words are vastly easier to remember than digits — to recall the number, just sound the word back out.

Dataset

Encoding is phonetic via the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, so silent letters behave correctly: “knife” = 28 (n‑f), “thumb” = 13 (th‑m), “laugh” = 58 (l‑f). Candidate ranking blends word frequency (SUBTLEX‑US Zipf scale), concreteness norms (Brysbaert et al. 2014, 1–5), and part of speech — concrete, common nouns make the strongest mental images.
